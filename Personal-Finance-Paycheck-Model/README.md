# 📊 Personal Finance & Paycheck Model

An Excel-based financial model designed to estimate **biweekly take-home pay, payroll deductions, recurring expenses, and savings allocations**.

This project was built to practice financial modeling, budgeting, forecasting, and Excel automation using a real-world personal finance use case.

---

## 🎯 Project Overview

The model takes work schedule and income information and converts it into an estimated paycheck and personal cash-flow plan.

### Model Flow

```text
Work Hours & Pay Rate
        ↓
    Gross Pay
        ↓
Estimated Tax Withholding
        ↓
    Net Pay
        ↓
Recurring Expenses
        ↓
Available Cash
        ↓
Savings & Investment Allocation
```

---

## ⚙️ Key Features

* 🕐 **Work-hour tracking**

  * Clock-in / clock-out tracking
  * Lunch break deductions
  * Automatic calculation of hours worked

* 💵 **Paycheck estimation**

  * Gross pay calculation
  * Biweekly paycheck forecasting
  * Estimated take-home pay

* 🧾 **Payroll deductions**

  * Federal income tax
  * Social Security
  * Medicare
  * California income tax
  * California SDI

* 📋 **Budget allocation**

  * Recurring expense tracking
  * Percentage-based allocation
  * Available cash calculation

* 💰 **Savings planning**

  * Emergency fund
  * Car savings
  * Maintenance
  * Travel
  * Gifts
  * Other savings goals

* 📈 **Data visualization**

  * Savings allocation chart
  * Easy-to-read financial summary

## 💡 Business Use Case

Although this model was created for personal financial planning, the underlying concepts are applicable to business environments.

The project demonstrates how spreadsheet-based financial models can be used to:

* Forecast income and cash flow
* Automate repetitive calculations
* Model financial assumptions
* Analyze expenses
* Allocate resources based on predefined percentages
* Evaluate different financial scenarios
* Present financial information in a clear format

These concepts are commonly used in areas such as **FP&A, financial analysis, budgeting, and business analysis**.

---

## 📊 Example Analysis

The model can be used to answer questions such as:

> **"If my hours or hourly rate change, how does that affect my take-home pay and savings?"**

> **"How much of each paycheck should be allocated toward expenses versus savings?"**

> **"How much can I contribute to different financial goals each pay period?"**

Because the model is formula-driven, changing an input automatically updates the downstream calculations.
