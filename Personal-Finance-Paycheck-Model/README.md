Personal Finance & Paycheck Modeling

An Excel-based financial model designed to estimate biweekly take-home pay, payroll tax deductions, recurring expenses, and savings allocations.

Automated gross and net pay calculations
Biweekly paycheck forecasting
Payroll tax estimation
Expense and savings allocation
Percentage-based budgeting
